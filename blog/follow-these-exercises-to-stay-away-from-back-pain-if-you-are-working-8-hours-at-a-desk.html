Working 8+ Hours at a Desk? Follow These Exercises to Stay Away from Back Pain

Raj Vishnu Kumar,   SJ Physiotherapy Team 4 min read

Picture this: You log in at 9 AM, dive into back-to-back calls or code and before you even realize it, it is 6 PM. Sound familiar?

Sitting in a seated position for 8 hours continuously has become the default routine for thousands of working professionals today. While you are busy getting work done, your spine takes a quiet hit. Continuous sitting tightens your hips, stiffens your lower back and rounds your shoulders forward - slowly turning end-of-day tiredness into persistent back pain.

The good news? You do not need hours in a gym or fancy equipment to fix this. Taking just 5 minutes between work to do these simple mobility exercises can keep your spine happy and pain-free:

4 Quick Exercises to Try at Your Desk

1

Seated Cat-Cow Stretch

Sit upright on your chair with feet flat on the floor and hands on your knees. Inhale, gently arch your back and look up toward the ceiling. Exhale, round your spine and look down toward your belly. Repeat 6 to 8 times. This instantly relaxes a stiff lower back.

2

Chest Opener & Shoulder Rolls

Roll your shoulders backward in big, slow circles 10 times. Next, clasp your hands behind your back, gently pull your shoulders open and lift your chest. Hold for 15 seconds. This simple move fights off the habit of slouching over your laptop.

3

Seated Figure-4 Hip Stretch

Cross your right ankle over your left knee to form a figure "4". Keeping your back straight, lean slightly forward until you feel a comfortable stretch in your hip and glutes. Hold for 20 seconds, then switch sides. Tight hips are a major hidden cause of lower back strain!

4

Standing Micro-Backbend

Whenever you stand up to grab a cup of water or chai, place your hands on your lower back and gently lean backward for 3 to 5 seconds. Repeat 3 times. It helps reset your spinal posture after hours of forward bending.

A Golden Habit to Remember

Try the 45-Minute Rule: Stand up or take a 60-second walk every 45 minutes. Movement is the best medicine for your body!

Final Thoughts

Back pain should never be the tax you pay for working hard. Small daily movements can make a massive difference. However, if you are already dealing with sharp pain or stiffness that just won't go away, it is wise not to ignore it.
