Shoulder Pain Treatment in Coimbatore: How Physiotherapy Helps

Shoulder pain can make everyday activities such as lifting your arm, getting dressed, reaching overhead and sleeping difficult. If shoulder discomfort is affecting your daily routine, physiotherapy for shoulder pain in Coimbatore may help improve mobility, manage pain and support recovery.

At SJ Physiotherapy, Singanallur, Coimbatore, we provide personalised physiotherapy care to help you move comfortably and return to your daily activities.

Common Causes of Shoulder Pain

Shoulder pain can develop due to injuries, repetitive movements, muscle strain, or underlying conditions. Common causes include:

Frozen Shoulder: Pain and stiffness that restrict shoulder movement.

Rotator Cuff Injuries: Problems affecting the muscles and tendons that support shoulder movement.

Muscle Strain: Overstretching or overloading the shoulder muscles.

Tendon Problems: Irritation that causes pain during arm movements.

Identifying the cause of shoulder pain is important for choosing the right treatment.

How Can Physiotherapy Help Shoulder Pain?

Shoulder physiotherapy focuses on reducing discomfort, improving flexibility and restoring shoulder function. Depending on your condition, a physiotherapist may recommend:

Assessment of shoulder movement and strength.

Appropriate mobility and strengthening exercises.

Manual therapy, when indicated.

Posture correction and activity modification.

Guidance on managing pain and preventing further irritation.

A personalised treatment plan can help improve shoulder mobility and support recovery.

When Should You Consult a Physiotherapist?

If shoulder pain persists, limits your arm movement, affects your sleep, or interferes with daily activities, consider consulting a physiotherapist. Sudden severe pain or pain following a significant injury requires prompt medical assessment.
